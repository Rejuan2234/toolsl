What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is an online date calculation tool that determines the amount of time between a person's date of birth and a selected date.

Most age calculators provide the result in years, months, and days. Some calculators may also provide additional information such as total days, total weeks, or total months.

The main purpose of an age calculator is to make age calculation quick and convenient without requiring manual date calculations.

How Does an Age Calculator Work?

The basic process is simple. The calculator compares two dates:

  • Your date of birth
  • The current date or another selected date

It then calculates the difference between those dates and displays the result in a human-readable format.

For example, if a person was born on January 10, 2005, and the calculation date is January 10, 2026, the person's age is exactly 21 years.

If the calculation date is different from the birthday, the result may include years, months, and days.

How to Calculate Your Exact Age Online

  3. Select the calculation date if the tool provides that option.
